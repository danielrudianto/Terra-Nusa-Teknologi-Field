# Nama rilis (Play Console, maks 50 karakter):
1.0.31 (64) — Susunan tremie di pantau cor

<id>
• Pantau cor menampilkan susunan tremie: jumlah batang, panjang tiap batang, berapa meter yang masih di lubang, dan batang yang sudah dicabut setelah TM keberapa.
• Riwayat titik: berhenti yang melewati malam hanya dihitung sampai jam 18.00 bila bor baru lanjut besoknya — sama dengan rekap lapangan.
</id>
<en-US>
• Watching a cast now shows the tremie string: how many pieces, each length, how many metres are still in the hole, and which pieces were pulled after which TM.
• Point history: a stop running overnight only counts until 18:00 when drilling resumes the next day — same as the field recap.
</en-US>

• 桩点历史：若次日才继续钻进，跨夜停机只计到 18:00——与现场汇总一致。
